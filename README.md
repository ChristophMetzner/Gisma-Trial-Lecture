## Mammogram paradox
Here is the mammogram paradox from the lecture.

### The Scenario
A 40-year-old woman tests positive on a mammogram. The test has 90% sensitivity and 91% specificity. Breast cancer prevalence at this age is about 1%. For simplicity, we assume a population of 1,000,000.

What is the probability she actually has cancer?
(a) ~90%     (b) ~75%     (c) ~50%     (d) ~9%
